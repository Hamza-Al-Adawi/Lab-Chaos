*Lab Chaos* is a chemistry lab simulator where you experiment with elements and compounds and watch reactions play out in real time. You stop seeing "just water" or "just glass" — you see the hydrogen and oxygen, the atoms and reactions that make them up.

Chemistry was always hard for me until I could practice it hands-on like this. The app includes built-in guidance and multiple levels, taking you from basics to advanced concepts — great for both school and university students.
